Exponential distribution: every message gets an extra delay of Exp(mean); by default each directed link stays FIFO (like TCP) — jitter only delays, it never reorders.
Packet loss and reordering
Messages picked by the share get an extra delay, uniform between the lower and upper bounds. Only with "Allow reordering" can they be overtaken by later sends; under FIFO the whole link just gets slower. Loss and reordering apply to every protocol message, including RPC requests and replies.
